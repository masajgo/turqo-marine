About Turqo Marine

An independent bridge between the yard floor and the boardroom.

Turqo Marine is a boutique maritime advisory firm based in İzmir, Turkey. We serve shipowners, cash buyers, brokers, and recycling yards across the full lifecycle of ship recycling — from pre-sale planning and compliance strategy through to final documentation and closure.

Our team combines operational depth from years on the yard floor with the regulatory and commercial fluency demanded by international clients. We do not own or operate yards, which means our advice is never shaped by commercial interests beyond yours.
