Sector Context

Turkey is no longer optional in green ship recycling.

The Hong Kong Convention entered into force on 26 June 2025. Combined with the EU Ship Recycling Regulation, the global pool of viable yards has narrowed sharply. Turkey — and specifically Aliağa — holds one of the largest concentrations of EU-approved facilities outside the EU itself.
